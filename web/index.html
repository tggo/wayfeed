The local news along your route, in your language.

Mark where you are going and when. Wayfeed reads what the country is saying about each stop and each day, translates it, and links the original.

  • SafetyProtests, strikes, curfews, floods near where you will be.
  • Roads and transportClosures, rail and bus strikes, landslides, airport trouble.
  • EventsFestivals, concerts and markets on your dates that fit what you like.
  • BackgroundWhat the city is talking about, so the place makes sense.

What to expect

Every item is translated and shows the source it came from, so you can check it yourself. Wayfeed collects reports; it is not an official warning service. Your trip is private and deleted after you travel.
