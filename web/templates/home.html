How it works

  1. Sign in with feedql. Wayfeed has no accounts of its own. feedql asks you to allow it to create and read one trip for you, and nothing else.
  2. Mark your stops on the map with dates. Before anything is saved you see what will be collected, who can see it and when it is deleted.
  3. Read the briefing for each stop, and get alerts if something serious is reported where you are. Alerts are what sources published, not an official warning.
